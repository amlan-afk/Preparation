# Interview story bank

Prepare concise stories with enough technical detail for follow-ups. Keep each story factual and separate your actions from the team's work.

## Story inventory

| Story | Competencies shown | Evidence/status |
|---|---|---|
| Production incident | debugging, ownership, reliability | To fill |

## Story template

### [Story title]

- **Situation:** What system or user context mattered?
- **Task:** What were you responsible for?
- **Action:** What did you personally do? Include investigation, choices, communication, and implementation.
- **Result:** What changed? Add a metric only if you can substantiate it.
- **Technical deep dive:** Architecture, tools, tests, rollout, trade-offs.
- **Follow-up questions:** What might an interviewer probe?
- **Lesson / prevention:** What changed afterward?
- **Ownership boundary:** Which parts belonged to others?
- **Version:** 30-second / 2-minute / deep-dive practiced?
