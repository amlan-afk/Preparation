# SDE 2 Job Switch HQ

A practical workspace for preparing for a full-stack SDE 2 move, based on the supplied preparation guide. The central aim is to defend every resume claim with clear technical depth, evidence, and ownership.

## Start here

1. Add your current resume to `01-profile/resume-source.md` (or paste its text there).
2. Break every resume bullet into an entry in `02-resume-mastery/bullet-interrogation.md`.
3. Follow the 10-week sequence in `03-roadmap/10-week-roadmap.md`, adapting the weekly load to your real availability.
4. Track practice and applications in `04-trackers/`.
5. Capture production, project, and collaboration stories in `05-interview-stories/`.

## Workspace map

- `01-profile/` — target roles, constraints, positioning, resume source.
- `02-resume-mastery/` — project architecture and bullet-by-bullet interview defense.
- `03-roadmap/` — 10-week plan and repeatable weekly routine.
- `04-trackers/` — study, DSA, mock, and application trackers.
- `05-interview-stories/` — STAR-style story bank and incident template.
- `06-question-bank/` — prioritized technical and behavioral prompts.
- `07-system-design/` — design framework and practice prompts.
- `08-templates/` — reusable session, weekly review, and mock notes.
- `DSA/` — 250 curated topic-wise questions, 200 company-wise archives (537 CSVs), and tracker.

## Working rules

- Be precise about what you personally built, what others owned, and what you can verify.
- Prefer depth on your actual React/TypeScript → Spring Boot → REST/GraphQL work over shallow coverage of every technology.
- For DSA, explain the baseline, optimization, invariant, complexity, and edge cases aloud.
- Keep an evidence field in stories and resume notes. Do not invent metrics or claim ownership you cannot support.
- Log applications and follow-ups promptly so preparation and job search stay visible together.
