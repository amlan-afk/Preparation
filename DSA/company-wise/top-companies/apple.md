## 🏢 Interview Format & Engineering Expectations

- **Interview Rounds:** 1-2 Recruiter & Technical Screens + 5-6 Team-specific Onsite rounds (Coding, Low-Level / High-Level Design, Domain Expertise)
- **High-Frequency Topics:** `Arrays, Strings, Hash Maps, Linked Lists, Trees, Heaps, Matrix Operations`
- **Company Context & Strategy:** Apple interviews are heavily team-dependent. Focus heavily on memory efficiency, cache-friendly data structures, pointer mechanics, and clean OOP architecture.

## 💡 How to Prepare for Apple

1. **Master the Top 20 First:** Over 50% of phone screen questions at Apple come directly from the top 20 frequency list above.
2. **Time Yourself:** Strict 20-minute timer per medium question. Practice articulating your thought process before writing code.
3. **Handle Edge Cases Early:** Before writing logic, state: *"The edge cases I want to test are: empty array, all negative, duplicates, and large values."*
4. **Tie Code to Architecture:** Always state the space-time tradeoff and discuss how this scales when data exceeds single-machine memory.
