## 💡 How to Prepare for Goldman Sachs

1. **Master the Top 20 First:** Over 50% of phone screen questions at Goldman Sachs come directly from the top 20 frequency list above.
2. **Time Yourself:** Strict 20-minute timer per medium question. Practice articulating your thought process before writing code.
3. **Handle Edge Cases Early:** Before writing logic, state: *"The edge cases I want to test are: empty array, all negative, duplicates, and large values."*
4. **Tie Code to Architecture:** Always state the space-time tradeoff and discuss how this scales when data exceeds single-machine memory.
