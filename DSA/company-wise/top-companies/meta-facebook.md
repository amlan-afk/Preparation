## 🏢 Interview Format & Engineering Expectations

- **Interview Rounds:** 1 Technical Screen (2 Mediums in 45 min) + 4 Onsite rounds (2 Coding rounds, 1 System Design, 1 Behavioral)
- **High-Frequency Topics:** `Strings, Two Pointers, Hash Maps, Binary Search, Trees, Graphs`
- **Company Context & Strategy:** Meta tests for speed, precision, and bug-free execution. You are expected to solve TWO LeetCode Mediums completely in 45 minutes (approx. 18-20 minutes per problem including dry-run).

## 💡 How to Prepare for Meta (Facebook)

1. **Master the Top 20 First:** Over 50% of phone screen questions at Meta (Facebook) come directly from the top 20 frequency list above.
2. **Time Yourself:** Strict 20-minute timer per medium question. Practice articulating your thought process before writing code.
3. **Handle Edge Cases Early:** Before writing logic, state: *"The edge cases I want to test are: empty array, all negative, duplicates, and large values."*
4. **Tie Code to Architecture:** Always state the space-time tradeoff and discuss how this scales when data exceeds single-machine memory.
