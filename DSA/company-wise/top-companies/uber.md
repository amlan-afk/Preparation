## 🏢 Interview Format & Engineering Expectations

- **Interview Rounds:** 1 Technical Screen + 4-5 Onsite rounds (2 Coding rounds, 1-2 System Design, 1 Culture / Collaboration)
- **High-Frequency Topics:** `Graphs (Shortest Path, Grid BFS), Heaps, Interval Merging, Dynamic Programming, Rate Limiters`
- **Company Context & Strategy:** Uber questions frequently reflect real ride/delivery logistics: routing, interval overlaps (driver shifts), bipartite matching, and rate limiting.

## 💡 How to Prepare for Uber

1. **Master the Top 20 First:** Over 50% of phone screen questions at Uber come directly from the top 20 frequency list above.
2. **Time Yourself:** Strict 20-minute timer per medium question. Practice articulating your thought process before writing code.
3. **Handle Edge Cases Early:** Before writing logic, state: *"The edge cases I want to test are: empty array, all negative, duplicates, and large values."*
4. **Tie Code to Architecture:** Always state the space-time tradeoff and discuss how this scales when data exceeds single-machine memory.
