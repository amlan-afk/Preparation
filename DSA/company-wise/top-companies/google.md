## 🏢 Interview Format & Engineering Expectations

- **Interview Rounds:** 1-2 Phone Screens + 4-5 Onsite rounds (3-4 Coding rounds, 1 System Design / Googlyness)
- **High-Frequency Topics:** `Graphs (Dijkstra, Topological Sort, Disjoint Set), Dynamic Programming, Advanced Trees, Trie, Binary Search`
- **Company Context & Strategy:** Google rarely asks direct LeetCode clones; they present real-world scenarios that map to complex graph or dynamic programming models. Interviewers care deeply about identifying invariants, edge case rigor, and clean abstraction.

## 💡 How to Prepare for Google

1. **Master the Top 20 First:** Over 50% of phone screen questions at Google come directly from the top 20 frequency list above.
2. **Time Yourself:** Strict 20-minute timer per medium question. Practice articulating your thought process before writing code.
3. **Handle Edge Cases Early:** Before writing logic, state: *"The edge cases I want to test are: empty array, all negative, duplicates, and large values."*
4. **Tie Code to Architecture:** Always state the space-time tradeoff and discuss how this scales when data exceeds single-machine memory.
