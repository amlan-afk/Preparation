## 🏢 Interview Format & Engineering Expectations

- **Interview Rounds:** 1 Phone Screen (1-2 questions) + 4-5 Onsite Loop rounds (1 DSA + LP, 1 System Design + LP, 2 DSA + LP, 1 Bar Raiser)
- **High-Frequency Topics:** `Trees, Graphs (BFS/DFS), Greedy, Priority Queues, Hash Maps, Dynamic Programming`
- **Company Context & Strategy:** Amazon is renowned for testing BFS/DFS on trees/grids, Top-K elements with heaps, and sliding window. Every single coding round dedicates 20-25 minutes to Leadership Principles (STAR method).

## 💡 How to Prepare for Amazon

1. **Master the Top 20 First:** Over 50% of phone screen questions at Amazon come directly from the top 20 frequency list above.
2. **Time Yourself:** Strict 20-minute timer per medium question. Practice articulating your thought process before writing code.
3. **Handle Edge Cases Early:** Before writing logic, state: *"The edge cases I want to test are: empty array, all negative, duplicates, and large values."*
4. **Tie Code to Architecture:** Always state the space-time tradeoff and discuss how this scales when data exceeds single-machine memory.
