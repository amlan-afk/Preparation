## 🎯 Strategic Company Preparation Framework

### The 80 / 20 Company Rule
- In almost every company, **the top 25-30 most frequent questions account for >60% of all interview prompts**.
- When you have an interview lined up with a specific company in 1-2 weeks:
  1. Open their dedicated playbook in [`top-companies/`](./top-companies/) or the corresponding CSV in [`csv/`](./csv/).
  2. Filter by `6months` and `1year` files first to identify their current question rotation.
  3. Solve the top 20 questions in order of frequency.
  4. Cross-reference each question with its pattern guide in [`../topic-wise/`](../topic-wise/).
